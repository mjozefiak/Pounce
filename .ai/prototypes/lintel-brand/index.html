Lintel: brand in the app

Design proposal · source: brand decisions of 2026-11-04 + .uxproof/ · native components are represented in HTML, not reused · status: proposal, acceptance pending

The brand appears in three places only: one accent colour, the orbit mark (icon, menu bar, empty states) and motion in onboarding and HUDs. Everything else is system.

1 · Launcher panel · 760 pt glass

Empty query panel-empty

Suggested (⌘1–⌘5) above Recently Used. Placeholder says what can be searched. The insertion point uses the brand accent.

Results panel-results

Selected row = 14 % accent tint plus a 3 pt accent bar on the leading edge. Text stays primary, so contrast is the system’s. Matched letters are bold in brand.accent.text (the plain accent is only 3.2:1 on the selected row).

No results panel-noresults

The orbit mark is the one illustration. One sentence says what to try.

Action error panel-error

Error row uses status.error with a glyph, never colour alone. It says what happened and what to do.

2 · HUD · glass capsule 248 × 40

Recording hud-rec

Level bars use brand.accent. The × cancels.

Processing hud-proc

The brand caret blinks while text is cleaned. Reduce Motion: caret stays solid.

Done hud-ok

Check glyph uses status.ok.

Error hud-err

Message plus one link, as in the contract.

3 · Settings · system grouped Form, no glass, 600 pt

Ready settings-ready

StatusBanner anatomy from the contract: what works, one action.

Needs setup settings-setup

One primary action (accent fill, white text 5.0:1).

Error settings-error

What happened and what to do.

Loading settings-load

Progress is a 3 pt accent bar plus the percentage in text.

4 · Welcome flow · step list 190 + page 500 pt

Pending: introduction welcome-intro

The orbit mark opens the flow. On appear its core pulses once (Reduce Motion: none).

Pending: shortcut welcome-shortcut

Primary action is the only accent fill on the page.

Done: microphone allowed welcome-done

Done step shows a check in the list.

Permission denied: microphone welcome-denied

Explains the fix and offers Skip, so the flow never blocks.

5 · About · 600 pt

About Lintel about

Same notices box as today. New: one-line tagline.
