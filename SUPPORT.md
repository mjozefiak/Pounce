# Getting help

Ask questions and report problems in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/mjozefiak/lintel/issues/new/choose).
GitHub Discussions are not enabled.

Please include:

- your macOS version and Mac model,
- the Lintel version (shown in the About window),
- the steps that lead to the problem, and what you expected.

Do not paste clipboard contents, transcripts, notes or other private text.

Documentation: the [README](README.md), [docs/qa.md](docs/qa.md), [docs/perf.md](docs/perf.md) and
[docs/signing.md](docs/signing.md).

For security problems, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue.
